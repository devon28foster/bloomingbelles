Nice to meet you

I'm Teyrr — your full spectrum doula.

Founder of Blooming Belles Birthing Services, dedicated to walking with families through every kind of birth story, with steady hands and an open heart.

My story

Care rooted in listening first.

I became a doula because I believe every person deserves to feel informed, respected, and supported through pregnancy, birth, and beyond — no matter how their story unfolds. "Full spectrum" means my care isn't limited to one kind of birth: I'm here for home births and hospital births, joyful surprises and hard goodbyes, first babies and fifth babies.

My approach blends evidence-based education with genuine presence. I'll never tell you what to choose — I'll make sure you have what you need to choose confidently, and then I'll show up for whatever comes next.
