Questions I hear often

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "full spectrum doula" mean?

It means my support isn't limited to one type of birth outcome. I'm trained to support home births, hospital births, unmedicated and medicated births, as well as pregnancy loss and difficult diagnoses. Every family and every story gets the same steady care.

When should I book a doula?

Anytime! Many families reach out in their second trimester, but I've supported clients who booked just weeks before their due date. If you're wondering whether it's too late, it's probably not — reach out.

Do you work with hospitals, midwives, and OBs?

Yes. I work alongside your existing care team, not in place of it. I'll coordinate with your provider so everyone is on the same page about your preferences.

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Omaha, Nebraska, and support families locally in person, with virtual prenatal and postpartum sessions available for clients further away.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — every package can be split into a payment plan. We'll figure out what works during your consult.
